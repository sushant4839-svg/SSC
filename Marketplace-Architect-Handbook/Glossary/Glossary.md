# Complete Glossary

- **GMV**: Gross merchandise value transacted through the marketplace before refunds and adjustments.
- **Take rate**: Net revenue divided by GMV.
- **Liquidity**: Probability that qualified demand finds acceptable supply within the required time and quality constraints.
- **Disintermediation**: Participants bypassing the marketplace after discovery.
- **Contribution margin**: Revenue remaining after variable costs directly tied to transactions.
