# Case Study: Airbnb and Trust

Background: lodging marketplaces require strangers to share high-value physical spaces. Problem: users needed confidence before booking or hosting. Decision: invest in profiles, reviews, messaging, guarantees, support, and policy. Execution: Airbnb layered identity, reputation, payments, and dispute processes. Result: trust systems enabled global category expansion. Lessons Learned: trust is not a feature; it is a full operating system.
